About TinkerHub CET

A community built by
students, for students.

We are a group of makers, learners and dreamers, exploring technology to create a positive impact on campus and beyond.

Who We Are

Where curiosity meets
community.

TinkerHub CET is the student chapter of TinkerHub at the College of Engineering Trivandrum. We create a space where students of all years and streams can explore technology, build projects, and grow together.

Our community runs on the belief that the best learning happens when you build something real — with people who care. We run workshops, hackathons, open sessions, and everything in between.

How We Operate

The TinkerHub CET Way

Four simple ideas that guide everything we do as a community.

01

Learn

Keep learning. Keep exploring. There's no ceiling on what you can discover when you're curious.

02

Build

Turn ideas into reality. We believe the best way to learn is to build something real — even if it breaks.

03

Share

Knowledge is better when shared. What you know can change someone else's journey.

04

Grow

Grow together as a community. Individual growth multiplies when we rise as a collective.

Community DNA

The TinkerHub Culture

Not unwritten rules — these are the shared traditions and campus energy that make TinkerHub CET feel like home.

#RITUAL_01

☕ Chai & Code

Saturday evenings around the campus canteen stairs where laptops come out, hot tea is shared, and tough bugs get squashed collaboratively across batches.

"Tea fuels the code."
#PHILOSOPHY

🧱 The Failure Wall

Broken circuits, crashed servers, and rejected ideas aren't hidden — we celebrate them. The fastest way to learn is breaking things with friends.

"Break it to make it."
#INCLUSIVITY

🚪 Zero Gatekeeping

Never opened a terminal before? Don't know how git push works? Perfect. Nobody is born a developer, and every expert here started from zero.

"All branches welcome."
#HACK_MODE

🌙 Midnight Buildathons

When hackathon deadlines approach: shared playlists, pizza boxes, whiteboards covered in diagrams, and the electric buzz of shipping a prototype before dawn.

"Shipped before sunrise."
#SHOWCASE

🎤 Unfiltered Show & Tell

Every month, members show off raw, unfinished side-projects. No polished slides required — just show what you're tinkering with and get instant support.

"Show what you tinker."
#INTERACTIVE

⚡ The Tinker Lab

Explore our interactive command palette, retro arcade mini-games, and hidden terminal easter eggs built directly into this digital home.

"More open.
More creative.
More inclusive."

— TinkerHub CET
