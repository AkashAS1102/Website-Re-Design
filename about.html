About TinkerHub CET

A community built by
students, for students.

We are a group of makers, learners and dreamers, exploring technology to create a positive impact on campus and beyond.

Who We Are

Where curiosity meets
community.

TinkerHub CET is the student chapter of TinkerHub at the College of Engineering Trivandrum. We create a space where students of all years and streams can explore technology, build projects, and grow together.

Our community runs on the belief that the best learning happens when you build something real — with people who care. We run workshops, hackathons, open sessions, and everything in between.

How We Operate

The TinkerHub CET Way

Four simple ideas that guide everything we do as a community.

01

Learn

Keep learning. Keep exploring. There's no ceiling on what you can discover when you're curious.

02

Build

Turn ideas into reality. We believe the best way to learn is to build something real — even if it breaks.

03

Share

Knowledge is better when shared. What you know can change someone else's journey.

04

Grow

Grow together as a community. Individual growth multiplies when we rise as a collective.
