Digital Vault

The TinkerHub Time Capsule

Relics, stickers, inside jokes, and artifacts that capture seven years of community memories.

ARTIFACT #01
FOUNDING · 2017

🏷️ The Original Vinyl Sticker

Hand-cut vinyl stickers distributed during our very first meet at the CET diamond jubilee stage. Some are still stuck to library laptops.

ARTIFACT #02
HACKATHON · 2019

👕 Drishti '19 Hackathon Tee

The midnight-black edition crewneck printed for our 48-hour hackathon. The back lists all 50 participant team names in monospace.

ARTIFACT #03
LORE · 2021

⚡ The Infamous 3:42 AM Commit

Commit message: "it compiles and nobody breathe or touch anything". Shipped 18 minutes before competition submission.

ARTIFACT #04
LOCKDOWN ERA · 2020

💬 Discord Voice Room #001

The 24/7 virtual study room that stayed connected through the pandemic, hosting over 1,200 hours of peer coding sessions.

ARTIFACT #05
LAB PROTOTYPE · 2022

🔌 The Burnt ESP32 Breadboard

Our first connected IoT sensor setup in the department lab. Smelled strongly of melted plastic, but transmitted telemetry flawlessly.

CURRENT · 2026
REVOLUTION · 2026

🚀 Drishti 2026 Redesign

The digital home reimagined from the ground up with interactive terminal tools, Maker Kootam archives, and full student ownership.
